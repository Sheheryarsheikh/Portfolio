Executive Sales & Operations Dashboard

Tools Used: Tableau Desktop, SQL, Excel  |  Target Audience: Operations Management Team

Business Case Study

1. The Business Problem

Regional sales dropped by 14% in Q3, and leadership lacked visibility into whether the issue stemmed from pricing mismatches, inventory bottlenecks, or regional shipping delays.

2. Key Insights Found

Data modeling revealed shipping transit times over 4 days directly caused a 40% drop in customer re-order rates. The Midwest hub was identified as the primary operational bottleneck.

3. Action & Impact

Recommended reallocating 15% of the logistics budget to regional micro-fulfillment centers. This operational change is projected to reduce transit times by 36% and restore Q4 churn numbers.
